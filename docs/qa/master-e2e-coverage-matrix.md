# Master E2E Coverage Matrix

Audit snapshot: 2026-10-25. This matrix reports retained prior QA evidence and source-backed current status; no tests were run for this inventory. Levels are **mutually exclusive highest evidenced level** per domain: L0 none; L1 static/code review only; L2 unit/UI component; L3 real MySQL integration; L4 real Android or Chromium E2E; L5 full relevant cross-system journey with MySQL reconciliation. A level measures evidence reached for a scoped journey, not completeness of the domain. `Y` flags mean at least one relevant scenario exists, not that all listed scenarios passed.

Totals reconcile to 39 domain rows: L5 8, L4 12, L3 7, L2 6, L1 5, L0 1. Automated-test evidence L2+ = 33; real MySQL L3+ = 27; Android E2E = 14; Admin Chromium E2E = 14; reconciled L5 = 8. Android and Chromium sets overlap in 8 domains; 20 domains have at least one of those UI channels, leaving 19 with neither.

## Coverage status interpretation

- **PASS** is reserved for the cited scenario/result in its source report; it is not inherited by unrun scenarios in the same domain.
- **BLOCKED** means a named approval, policy, environment or safe fixture is missing. **NOT RUN** means it was not executed; **N/A** means the scenario does not apply to the identified implementation.
- L5 examples are explicitly scoped. They do not close Module 02, 04B, 04C or 04D globally, and they do not imply live financial settlement.
- All module-by-module current status remains as recorded in [module-status.md](module-status.md); this inventory does not rewrite historical evidence.
